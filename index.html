01
July 11 — The beginning

Our First Meeting 🛕

I met you at a temple, Athan. July 11 will always be a special date in my heart. I never knew that one simple meeting would become the beginning of such a beautiful story. We didn't take a photograph that day. But maybe that's okay. Some moments don't need a camera to become unforgettable.

"I may not have a picture of that day, but I will always have the memory of you. That moment when I saw you, I felt something special and butterflies everywhere." ❤️

02
After our first meeting

Our First Phone Calls 📞

After we met, we started talking on the phone. Those simple calls slowly became something I looked forward to. Hearing your voice made ordinary days feel a little more special. Every day started with our conversations. But August 3 was different. That was the day I didn't hear your voice. I felt something was missing. I waited for your call for almost five hours. I texted you and called you, but there was no response. I thought maybe this was your revenge for me not picking up your call on Saturday, August 1. If I hurt you, I'm sorry for everything.

"Somehow, your voice became one of my favourite sounds." ❤️

03
July 22 — A special moment

Our First Video Call 📱

July 22 was our first video call! Until then, I had only heard your voice. But that day, I finally got to see your face while talking to you. Slowly, your calls became my happiness, your voice became my favourite sound, and seeing your face became something I always waited for.

"From hearing your voice to seeing your smile, every little moment made me fall for you a little more." ❤️

04
August 31 — Our first picture

Our First Photo Together 📸

Finally, a picture of us together! August 31 was such a beautiful day. We went to RmKV for our engagement saree shopping. But honestly, my favourite part wasn't the shopping. It was spending that day with you.

We sat together and had lunch together. That simple lunch became one of my most unforgettable memories. I don't know whether you remember every little moment of that day, but I will always remember it. Sitting beside you, sharing food, laughing and spending time together made me so happy.

"My favourite picture isn't just a photograph. It's a memory of the day I got to sit beside you and enjoy every little moment." ❤️

05
The first gift from you

My First Gift 🎁

✨💎✨

Your first gift to me came through your mom — a beautiful chimmiki kamal. ❤️

It wasn't just a pair of earrings. It became a little reminder of you, your thoughtfulness and the love behind that gesture.

"My first gift from my love — a memory I will always treasure." ❤️

06
September 13 — Our special day

Our Engagement Day 💍

💍❤️💍

September 13 — the day we officially became engaged! It was such a hectic day, wasn't it, Athan? So many things happening, so many people, so much running around and so many emotions. But somewhere between all that chaos, we created some of our most beautiful memories. Our beautiful photographs, our smiles, the excitement and the feeling of becoming a part of each other's lives made that day unforgettable.

"Among all the beautiful moments of that day, my favourite one was knowing that I was going to spend my life with you." ❤️

07
A moment I will never forget

Our First Kiss 💋

❤️😘

And here comes one of my most unforgettable memories! It happened on our engagement day. We were sitting together, and suddenly you kissed me! And the funniest and most unforgettable part? It happened right in front of my dad! 🙈

I was so surprised, happy, shy and scared at the same time. I don't know whether you remember that moment the way I do, but I will always remember it. It was unexpected, a little scary, but one of the most beautiful memories of my life.

"A little unexpected, a little scary, but completely beautiful. My first kiss with you will always be special." ❤️

08
Not always perfect, but always special

Our Little Fights ❤️

Our story isn't always filled with romantic moments. We have our little fights, misunderstandings, silly arguments, teasing and sometimes those moments when neither of us wants to talk first. But between all those little fights, there is also so much love, care, affection and understanding. I know we are not perfect. We both make mistakes, and sometimes we hurt each other without intending to.

"I don't want a perfect love story. I just want a story where we never stop choosing each other." ❤️

09
October 25 — Another beautiful memory

Our Wedding Saree Shopping 💗

October 25 was our wedding saree shopping day! Athan, I know how hectic and crowded the shop was. You were getting so angry and tense because there were so many people around us. But do you know what I remember the most?

The way you touched me, the way you placed your hands on my shoulder, and the way you held my hand and took me towards the silver section. Those little gestures made me feel so loved and special. I loved the way you held my hand. I loved the way you pulled me along with you. Maybe those things seemed ordinary to you, but they meant so much to me.

My Special Silver Ring 💍

And then you bought me a silver ring! It wasn't just a ring. It became another little memory of us. Whenever I look at it, I will remember that crowded shop, your hand holding mine, and that beautiful little moment.

"It's not about how expensive a gift is. It's about the person who gives it and the beautiful memory attached to it." ❤️

And there is one thing I want you to know...
I LOVE YOU
ATHAN ❤️

From our first meeting on July 11, to our first video call on July 22, our first photograph on August 31, our engagement on September 13, our unexpected first kiss, our little fights and endless love, and our wedding shopping on October 25... Every little moment has become a beautiful chapter of our story.

Athan, you are more important to me than you probably realise.

I know you have work, responsibilities and so many things to take care of. I understand that you cannot always be available. I never expect you to spend every minute with me. I don't want to disturb your work or your busy days.

But sometimes, just remember that there is one girl waiting for your call, your message and even a small text from you.

A simple message like "Had lunch?", "Reached home", "I'm busy right now", or even "Something came up, I'll let you know later" is enough to make me feel included in your day.

Do you remember how you were in the beginning? How you used to call, message and tell me even the smallest things? I miss that sometimes. I know relationships change with time, but I don't want us to lose those little things that made me feel so loved in the beginning.

Sometimes your changes hurt me. Not because I want all your attention, but because I miss the way you used to make me feel like I was a part of everything.

I don't want to be just another person in your busy day. I want to be someone special, someone you remember even when life gets busy, someone you want to share your little moments with.

I don't need expensive gifts, big surprises or hours of conversation. Sometimes, all I need is a little time, a little attention and the same love you showed me in the beginning.

We have so many beautiful memories behind us and so many beautiful moments waiting ahead. I want more photographs, more lunches together, more shopping days, more silly fights, more kisses and more reasons to smile.

I love you, Athan. ❤️

Please don't forget the girl who is always waiting to hear from you.
